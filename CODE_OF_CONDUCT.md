# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The PSX Classic Modding Hub is intended to be a professional, technically focused, and respectful community for collaboration around PlayStation Classic modding, RetroArch configurations, controller profiles, compatibility testing, hardware documentation, and related tooling.

Everyone participating in this repository is expected to communicate constructively, respect other contributors, and keep discussions focused on technical facts and reproducible results.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally
- provide technically accurate and reproducible information whenever possible
- accept constructive review and correction
- distinguish verified test results from assumptions or untested claims
- respect different levels of experience and knowledge
- keep Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, and comments relevant to the repository
- disclose potential conflicts, limitations, hardware revisions, and test conditions when they materially affect a contribution
- respect licenses, copyrights, trademarks, privacy, and third-party rights
- use only systems, software, files, hardware, and content they are authorized to use
- follow the repository's contribution, security, and issue-reporting procedures

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct
- personal attacks or deliberately hostile communication
- spam, repeated off-topic content, or disruptive posting
- knowingly submitting false, fabricated, or misleading technical information
- presenting untested controller profiles, compatibility claims, or configurations as verified
- publishing private information or personal data without permission
- publishing credentials, tokens, private keys, or other sensitive information
- distributing or requesting copyrighted ROM images, BIOS files, game files, piracy resources, or other unauthorized copyrighted content
- encouraging unauthorized access, destructive behavior, malware distribution, or misuse of security information
- intentionally bypassing repository contribution or security-reporting processes
- abusing Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, or discussions to target individuals or organizations

## Technical Contributions

Technical claims should be supported by sufficient context. Depending on the contribution, this can include:

- exact hardware or device identification
- Vendor-ID and Product-ID
- RetroArch and Core versions
- PSX Classic setup details
- configuration parameters
- reproducible test steps
- tested games or workloads
- known limitations or hardware revisions

Contributions that cannot be reproduced, are materially incomplete, or are based only on assumptions may be requested for revision or closed.

## Security Issues

Security-sensitive information must not be published through public Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, or comments.

Please follow `SECURITY.md` and report security concerns privately to:

**bylicki@mail.de**

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, remove, lock, or close content that violates this Code of Conduct or other repository policies. Depending on severity or repeated violations, contributors may also be restricted from further participation.

Enforcement decisions should be based on the content and behavior involved, the impact on the project and community, and whether the behavior is repeated or deliberate.

## Reporting Conduct Problems

If a conduct problem cannot reasonably be handled in public, it may be reported privately to:

**bylicki@mail.de**

Please include the relevant GitHub URL, usernames involved, a concise description of the incident, and any supporting context that is necessary to understand the report.

Reports should be made in good faith. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in the PSX Classic Modding Hub repository, including Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, comments, repository-related discussions, and other project communication channels controlled by the maintainers.

## Project Principle

The project values verifiable technical work over speculation, respectful collaboration over confrontation, and responsible modding over unsafe or unauthorized activity.
